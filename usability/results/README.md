[pilot 1](../prolific_pilot1_results.md) and
[pilot 2](../prolific_pilot2_results.md). They used general adult samples, not a
verified sample of research-data curators, and do not validate leak detection.

collected. Synthetic reports and scorer tests may be regenerated, but the
first completed response set must be preserved unchanged.

as though that specification were the frozen web-pilot protocol. The local web
follow-up plan used a ten-person target with separate C/D and integrity targets;
its provenance limitations are described in the pilot-2 result.
