# Prolific pilot 2 results

I ran a second small web pilot after revising the release decision and integrity
wording that caused problems in the first sample.

## What was tested

- 8 complete participants from a new general adult sample;
- 10 reports built from synthetic metadata;
- 13 fixed-choice questions and one short explanation per participant;
- 112 answers in total.

The study stopped with eight complete responses rather than the planned ten.
All eight submissions were reviewed and paid. Curator experience was not
required or measured, so this remains formative evidence rather than the
intended evaluation with research-data staff or neurodata curators.

The analysis used a private pseudonymous export. It contains no Prolific ID,
session ID or stored identifier hash. Row-level responses are not published.

## Main result

- fixed-choice accuracy: **99/104 (95.2%)**;
- critical release and integrity decisions: **45/48 (93.8%)**;
- short explanations meeting both rubric elements: **8/8**;
- median task time: **13:34**;
- range: **10:03–18:47**;
- mean confidence: **4.47/5**.

Every participant completed all fourteen tasks.

## Interpretation

The changes to Reports C and D worked in this sample. The remaining errors were
concentrated in the distinction between Reports A and B, plus one error each on
Reports F and J. These are targets for small wording changes rather than a broad
redesign.

This result does **not** pass the original ten-person gate because only eight
people completed the clean study. It also does not establish expert usability,
anonymity, legal compliance or security certification.

The next meaningful test should use at least five people who have prepared EEG,
MEG, MRI or related research data for sharing. It should use the next frozen
report version and keep the same critical-decision thresholds.
